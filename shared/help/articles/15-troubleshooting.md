# Troubleshooting

## WhatsApp messages are not sending
- Meta needs a payment method on your WhatsApp Business account (Settings → Channels → WhatsApp Account Health → Open Meta Billing).
- The customer's 24-hour window is closed: only an approved template can be sent.
- The template is still Pending, or was Rejected (see the reason on the WhatsApp page). Press Sync Meta Templates for the real status.
- Quality rating dropped or the number is restricted (Settings → Channels → WhatsApp Account Health).
- The plan is paused (Billing page).

## The AI gave a wrong or no answer
- It answers only from your Business card, Catalogue, Question bank and knowledge entries. Correct or add the answer there; it is used from the next message.
- Check the Gaps queue for questions it couldn't answer.
- In Draft mode, reject the wrong draft with a reason.

## An automation didn't run
Open Automations → Activity. Each step shows ran / skipped (with the reason) / queued / failed. See the Automations article for the common causes (template not approved, quiet hours, daily cap, condition never matching, rule paused).

## Calls are not being placed or answered
- Wallet balance is zero (Billing → Wallet).
- No voice number connected yet, or KYC still in review (Voice page).
- The campaign purpose doesn't match your outbound number series (Settings → Calls & queue).

## I can't see a page or button
- The feature is off for your business: Settings → Business → Features.
- Your role: agents don't see Billing, Team, Settings, Analytics, Automations or Campaigns.
- No plan yet: WhatsApp, Instagram and Voice stay locked until the first payment.

## Can't sign in
Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. Team members sign in with their Team ID on the team sign-in page; the owner or an admin can reset their password on the Team page.

## Still stuck
Raise a ticket from the Help page. Describe what you did, what you expected and what happened instead; mention the page. KROVA's team replies on the Help page.
