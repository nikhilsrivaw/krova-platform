# Billing, plans and the wallet

The exact prices, limits and wallet amounts are listed under "Current plans and prices" below this article set - always quote those numbers.

## Plans
Four monthly plans: Solo Rs 1,699, Starter Rs 2,999, Pro Rs 7,999 and Scale Rs 12,499 (per month, plus 18% GST). They differ in size (customer messages a month the plan is built for, phone lines, team logins, how much business information the AI reads, campaign audience size, contacts per import, lead sources, API keys and webhooks). Solo is for one person: 500 customer messages, only the owner's login, 1 phone line, WhatsApp campaigns up to 300 people, imports (contacts and leads) up to 1,000 rows, no lead sources beyond your own forms - and it leaves out Call campaigns, Website chat, Feedback, Analytics reports, Ask KROVA (in the phone app and when the owner calls their own KROVA number - that call is answered like any customer's) and Teams and routing rules (they show a lock and open Billing). WhatsApp, Instagram, the Voice Agent, automations, WhatsApp campaigns and your business type's tools are on every plan, Solo too.

Customer messages a month: the owner and admins get a phone notification at 80% and at 100% of the plan's size, on every plan. Starter, Pro and Scale are never stopped. On Solo, once 550 messages are used (500 plus 10% extra) automatic WhatsApp and Instagram replies pause until the 1st of next month or a move to Starter: messages still arrive in the inbox and go to a person, and emergencies, requests for a person and feedback buttons are still answered. Calls are paid from the wallet and never counted. The Billing page shows this month's count. If you subscribed before 2 Nov 2026, you keep the price you signed up at; a new plan or a change of plan is at today's price.
- Only the owner can choose or change the plan (Billing page).
- You approve the monthly payment once (card or UPI AutoPay) and it renews by itself; you are told before each charge.
- If a renewal fails, it is retried over a week before the plan is paused. While paused, AI replies, calls and campaigns are off; your customers and conversations stay.
- Going past the message size of your plan doesn't switch anything off - moving up keeps the cost per message lower.
- Coupon code: enter it on the Billing page before choosing a plan.

## Wallet (for voice)
Calls and phone number rent are paid from the wallet, not the plan. Billing page → Wallet → Add money. GST and the payment gateway fee are added on top, so the wallet gets exactly the amount you chose. Calls stop when the balance reaches zero; you see a warning when it is running low.

## Invoices
Billing page → Invoices and receipts: a GST invoice after each plan payment, a receipt after each wallet top-up. Add your billing details (business name, address, GSTIN if registered, state) before your first payment so the invoice is right. Invoices are issued by Aqirox Technology.

## Payment problems
- "Payment did not go through": you were not charged; try again.
- "Your bank is still confirming": UPI can take a few minutes; the page updates by itself.
- "Online payments are not switched on for this workspace yet": raise a ticket.
