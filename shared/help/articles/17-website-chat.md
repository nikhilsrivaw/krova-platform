# Website chat (AI chat button on your own website)

Website chat page (sidebar, under Channels - owner and admins).

- Add website: type your site's address (for example myshop.in). The chat button works on that address and its www. form only, not on other sites. You can add up to 10 websites.
- Chat title, first message and colour: what visitors see on the chat button and window. Leave the title or first message empty to use "Chat with us" and "Hi! How can I help you today?".
- Copy the code and paste it just before </body> on every page of your website. WordPress, Wix, Shopify and most website builders have a "custom code", "footer scripts" or "theme code" box for this. A chat button appears at the bottom right.
- Try it here: opens the real chat on the Website chat page itself so you can test it before pasting the code. Reload the page to try again after changing settings.
- Chat is on: switch it off to hide the chat button on your website without removing the code. Remove website stops the code working at once; past website conversations stay in the Inbox.

What visitors get:
- A short notice first ("This chat is answered by an AI assistant...") and a Start Chat button - nothing they type is read before they press it.
- The AI answers from your Knowledge and can book appointments and tokens when Bookings or Queue are on. To book, it asks for their name and phone number.
- When the AI cannot help, or the visitor asks for a person, it asks for their name and phone number. Without a number nobody can reply, so these chats do not appear in the Inbox.

When a visitor leaves their number:
- The chat appears in the Inbox under that customer (joined with their WhatsApp history if they already wrote to you from the same number).
- The AI keeps answering. When it needs a person (it cannot help, or the visitor asks for one), the chat goes to someone the same way as other chats (Team page: teams, routing rules, round-robin) and that person gets a notification.
- Reply from the Inbox as usual. When the customer's last message came from the website chat, your reply goes to their chat window on your website ("Reply in their website chat") - they see it within a few seconds while the chat is open, or next time they open it, with a "Team" label. A red dot on the chat button shows a new reply.
- While a person is replying (a reply in the last 15 minutes), the AI stays quiet so the visitor does not get two answers, and their new messages notify whoever has the chat.
- You can reply on the website chat for up to 7 days after the visitor was last on it. After that, reach them on WhatsApp instead.
- The reply-time target (Team page) also counts website chats waiting for a person.

The chat button hides itself if your KROVA plan is paused.
