# Website chat (AI chat button on your own website)

Website chat page (sidebar, under Channels - owner and admins).

Setting it up:
- Add website: type your site's address (for example myshop.in). The chat button works on that address and its www. form only, not on other sites. You can add up to 10 websites.
- Copy the code and paste it just before </body> on every page of your website. The page shows the steps for WordPress, Wix and Shopify; most other website builders have a "custom code" or "footer code" box.
- "Working on your website" (green) means a page with the code opened the chat in the last two days. "Not seen on your website yet" means the code is not pasted, not published, or on a different address than the one you added.
- Try it here: opens the real chat on the Website chat page itself, so you can test before pasting the code. Reload the page to try again after changing settings.
- Chat is on: switch it off to hide the chat button on your website without removing the code. Remove website stops the code working at once; past website conversations stay in the Inbox.

What you can change (Edit website):
- Chat title, first message and colour, with a preview.
- Button side (right or left), theme (dark or light), and an optional label beside the button such as "Need help? Chat with us".
- Pop-up message: a short line that shows next to the button a few seconds after a visitor arrives, once a day, for visitors who have not chatted yet.
- Ready questions: up to four buttons ("What are your prices?", "Book an appointment") shown before the visitor types.
- Chat window language: English or Hindi for the buttons and notices. The AI answers in whatever language the visitor writes.
- Name and phone number: "Only when needed" (the AI asks when a person or a booking needs it) or "Ask before the chat starts" (every visitor who chats becomes a lead in your Inbox; some visitors leave instead).
- WhatsApp button: a button in the chat header that opens a WhatsApp chat with your connected WhatsApp number.

What visitors get:
- A short notice first ("This chat is answered by an AI assistant...") and a Start chat button - nothing they type is read before they press it.
- The AI answers from your Knowledge and can book appointments and tokens when Bookings or Queue are on. Links in a reply (a payment link, a map) can be tapped. A typing dot shows while the AI is answering.
- The chat stays as they move between pages of your website or reload the page. On a phone the chat opens full screen.
- When the AI cannot help, or the visitor asks for a person, it asks for their name and phone number. Without a number nobody can reply, so these chats do not appear in the Inbox - but you can still read them under Recent website chats.

When a visitor leaves their number:
- The chat appears in the Inbox under that customer (joined with their WhatsApp history if they already wrote to you from the same number).
- The AI keeps answering. When it needs a person, the chat goes to someone the same way as other chats (Team page: teams, routing rules, round-robin) and that person gets a notification.
- Reply from the Inbox as usual. When the customer's last message came from the website chat, your reply goes to their chat window ("Reply in their website chat") - they see it within a few seconds while the chat is open, or next time they open it, with a "Team" label. A red dot on the chat button shows a new reply.
- While a person is replying (a reply in the last 15 minutes), the AI stays quiet so the visitor does not get two answers, and their new messages notify whoever has the chat.
- You can reply on the website chat for up to 7 days after the visitor was last on it. After that, reach them on WhatsApp instead.
- The reply-time target (Team page) also counts website chats waiting for a person.
- For privacy, a visitor only ever sees this chat's own messages, never earlier conversations of the same number.

On the Website chat page:
- Last 7 days: chats started, how many left a number, how many needed a person.
- Recent website chats: every chat with its first question, website and page, including visitors who never left a number. Open one to read it; "Open in Inbox to reply" when the visitor left a number.

The chat button hides itself if your KROVA plan is paused.
