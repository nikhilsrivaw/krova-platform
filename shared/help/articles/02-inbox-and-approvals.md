# Conversations and Approvals

## Conversations (the inbox)
Conversations shows every customer's WhatsApp, Instagram and voice call history as one timeline. Search finds a customer or anything ever said. Filters: All, Mine, Unassigned.
- Reply: type your own reply, send a saved (canned) reply, or use Quick Send for tappable buttons, a picker list, a product or the catalog.
- Customer 360 (side panel): AI profile, open promises, notes for the team (the customer never sees them), tags, known phone numbers and IDs.
- Mark Private (owner and admins only): for personal chats. The AI never reads or answers a private chat, and team members (agents) can't see it at all.
- Call the customer: your phone rings first, then the customer is connected. You need to own (take over) the chat first.
- Book Token: when the walk-in queue is on and a shift is open.

## The 24-hour window
WhatsApp lets you send free text only within 24 hours of the customer's last message. After that the chat shows "24h window closed" and you can only send an approved template (see the WhatsApp article). Approval drafts whose window closed can no longer be sent.

## Who owns a chat
If "The first agent to reply owns the chat" is on (Team page), the first teammate who replies owns it. Others must press take over before they can reply. New chats can also be given out round-robin. Owners and admins always see every chat.

## Approvals
In Draft and Conditional mode, AI replies wait here with the AI's confidence, why it proposed the reply, and any gap it noticed.
- Approve & Send, Edit Before Send, or Reject (give a reason - it helps the AI avoid the same mistake).
- "AI could not answer - needs a person": the AI had nothing to answer from. Write the reply yourself, then add the answer to your Business card so it knows next time.
- If the AI also suggested a carousel, you can turn it off before sending.

## Auto-Send Rules
Approvals page → Auto-Send Rules. Used in Conditional mode. A reply only sends itself if it clears every rule: a minimum confidence, and none of your blocked phrases (for example refund, cancel, price change). Anything uncertain still lands in Approvals.
