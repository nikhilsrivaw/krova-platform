# Instagram and the Voice Agent

## Instagram
Instagram page → Connect with Instagram (sign in with Instagram). You need an Instagram Business or Creator account. Once connected, DMs and comments arrive in Conversations and the AI can reply.
- Send a message or a carousel by hand to someone who messaged you in the last 24 hours.
- Saved carousels: give a carousel a name and what it is for; the AI can then send it during a reply when a customer's message matches.
- Publish a photo, and see Insights (followers, profile views, engagement).
- Disconnect or Reconnect from the same page.

## Voice Agent: getting a phone number
Indian rules require carrier KYC before a number can be bought. On the Voice page → Regulatory KYC & Compliance:
1. Create Voice Subaccount
2. Register Identity (your business name)
3. Upload Documents (for example a GST certificate or Certificate of Incorporation / Udyam)
4. Submit for Review - status moves from In Review to Compliance Approved.
Then search a local number by area code or city (for example 080 Bengaluru, 022 Mumbai) and Buy & Connect. 140-series (promotional) and 160-series (BFSI only) numbers are not instant - request them on the Voice page; KROVA's team arranges them and updates the request.

## Voice Agent settings
Voice page → Number Settings (Agent Speech & Greeting card), per number:
- Greeting, agent voice (male/female), language: Adaptive (replies in whatever the caller speaks - Hindi, English or a mix) or Fixed.
- Owner Phone Number: calling your voice line from this number gets you an agent that answers about your own ledger (who owes you, what is overdue).
- Call Answering: AI Answers (default), or Live Copilot (a short greeting, then the "Who answers" list rings and suggestions show on screen).
- Several numbers: name each (e.g. "Sales"), choose incoming/outgoing use, and which one outgoing calls go from.

## Each number's own job (Voice page → Number Settings → "This number's job and who answers")
Pick the number at the top, then:
- What this number is for: Sales, Support, Bookings, Branch or Custom, plus a few lines in your own words (for example "Jaipur branch, open 10 to 7"). The AI is told this only on calls that come in on that number.
- Who answers: the people a call is put through to when the AI hands over or the caller presses 0. Up to 3 levels: level 1 rings first, then level 2, then level 3. Each level rings its people one by one or all together (whoever picks up first gets the call), for the seconds you set. Add team members (their phone comes from the Team page, and anyone marked Away is skipped) or any outside number with a name, like a reception landline. The customer's own team member always rings first. "Longest a caller waits" stops the ringing after that many seconds in total. If you leave the list empty, every team member with a phone rings one by one, as before.
- When nobody answers: "Promise a callback" (the caller hears when you will call back, a callback task goes on the Commitment Ledger with that due time, and the owner, admins and level-1 people get a notification), "Take a voicemail" (the recording link goes on the customer's notes and the callback task; Plivo keeps recordings 30 days), or "Just apologise".
- Test call: rings any phone from this number with a short recorded line, so you can check a number on the list really rings. 5 a day.
- Automations: rules for "A call finishes" and campaign voicemail/no-answer can have the condition "Number" = one of your numbers, so each number can have its own follow-ups.

## Giving a number back
"Release Number" first shows what still uses it: campaigns that call from it, automation rules that only run for it, and whether it is your default number for outgoing calls. Choose another number for those campaigns (or leave them without a number, and a running one is paused). Rules for that number are switched off, and the oldest remaining number becomes the default for outgoing calls. Call history stays. This month's rent is not refunded.

## Calls, scripts and costs
- Call Logs: every call with outcome, duration, summary and cost breakdown; open the full transcript in Conversations.
- Call Scripts: a fixed question list for lead qualification or a feedback survey, used by call campaigns.
- Calls are paid from your wallet (Billing page), not from the plan. Calls stop when the wallet reaches zero. Each phone number also has a monthly rent taken from the wallet.
