# Instagram and the Voice Agent

## Instagram
Instagram page → Connect with Instagram (sign in with Instagram). You need an Instagram Business or Creator account. Once connected, DMs and comments arrive in Conversations and the AI can reply.
- Send a message or a carousel by hand to someone who messaged you in the last 24 hours.
- Saved carousels: give a carousel a name and what it is for; the AI can then send it during a reply when a customer's message matches.
- Publish a photo, and see Insights (followers, profile views, engagement).
- Disconnect or Reconnect from the same page.

## Voice Agent: getting a phone number
Indian rules require carrier KYC before a number can be bought. On the Voice page → Regulatory KYC & Compliance:
1. Create Voice Subaccount
2. Register Identity (your business name)
3. Upload Documents (for example a GST certificate or Certificate of Incorporation / Udyam)
4. Submit for Review - status moves from In Review to Compliance Approved.
Then search a local number by area code or city (for example 080 Bengaluru, 022 Mumbai) and Buy & Connect. 140-series (promotional) and 160-series (BFSI only) numbers are not instant - request them on the Voice page; KROVA's team arranges them and updates the request.

## Voice Agent settings
Voice page → Agent Speech & Greeting, per number:
- Greeting, agent voice (male/female), language: Adaptive (replies in whatever the caller speaks - Hindi, English or a mix) or Fixed.
- Staff Phone Number: a real person the agent can bring onto a call when it escalates. Callers can press 0 any time to reach this number.
- Owner Phone Number: calling your voice line from this number gets you an agent that answers about your own ledger (who owes you, what is overdue).
- Call Answering: AI Answers (default), or Live Copilot (the call goes to your staff number and suggestions show on screen).
- Several numbers: name each (e.g. "Sales"), choose incoming/outgoing use, and which one outgoing calls go from.

## Calls, scripts and costs
- Call Logs: every call with outcome, duration, summary and cost breakdown; open the full transcript in Conversations.
- Call Scripts: a fixed question list for lead qualification or a feedback survey, used by call campaigns.
- Calls are paid from your wallet (Billing page), not from the plan. Calls stop when the wallet reaches zero. Each phone number also has a monthly rent taken from the wallet.
