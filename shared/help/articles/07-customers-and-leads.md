# Customers, Leads and Forms

## Customers
Customers page lists everyone who has messaged or called you, with a health score, open promises and amount outstanding.
- Open a customer (Inspect 360): AI profile, contact channels, pipeline stage, who pays for them (Paid By), key dates, deal value, Marketing Opt-In, tags and notes.
- Key dates: a renewal, AMC expiry or birthday. Tick "Comes every year" for yearly dates. Automations can act on them ("5 days before Renewal → WhatsApp").
- Tags: tag one customer, or select many and tag them together. Campaigns can target a tag.
- Pipeline: switch the Customers page to the board view and drag a customer to another stage. The stages come with your business type.
- Import Contacts: upload a CSV with a phone column (and optionally name). Export CSV downloads the list.
- Marketing Opt-In: only opted-in customers receive marketing templates.

## Leads
Leads page: every enquiry with its source and status - Justdial, IndiaMART, property portals, email, forms, file uploads.
- Connect a lead source: Settings → Leads & payments → Connect lead sources. Each source gets its own URL or forwarding address; generate it, copy it, and set it up on that platform. Generating a new one stops the old one working.
- Add leads by hand or upload a CSV/Excel exported from a portal (Settings → Leads & payments).
- To act on every new lead automatically (WhatsApp them, call them, alert the team), use the Ready-made "new lead" automation.

## Forms
Forms page (when Leads & forms is on): build a form, publish it to get a shareable link, and every submission becomes a lead. Fields can be on several pages and shown only if another field is ticked. Add a logo and accent colour.
