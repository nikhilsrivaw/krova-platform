# Getting started with KROVA

## What KROVA is
KROVA answers your customers on WhatsApp, Instagram and phone calls with AI, and keeps track of what was promised in those conversations (payments, callbacks, deliveries) in one place called the Commitment Ledger. Your team works the same inbox, and automations follow up for you.

## First steps after signing up
1. Pick what kind of business you are (Settings → Business). This decides which modules you start with, how the AI talks and what it must never promise. You can change it later.
2. Choose a plan on the Billing page. Until a plan is bought, you can look around (chats, customers, calendar), but WhatsApp, Instagram and the Voice Agent stay locked.
3. Connect WhatsApp (WhatsApp page → Connect WhatsApp Number). It opens Meta's own signup window.
4. Fill your Business card (Knowledge & Gaps page) - prices, hours, policies. The AI answers only from what you put there.
5. Decide how much the AI may send on its own (Settings → Agent Autonomy Level). Start with Draft Mode.

## How much the AI does on its own (autonomy levels)
Set in Settings → Business → Agent Autonomy Level.
- Observe Mode: the AI only reads and fills the ledger. It drafts no replies.
- Draft Mode (recommended): the AI writes a reply, a person approves, edits or rejects it on the Approvals page before it is sent.
- Conditional: replies that pass your own auto-send rules (Approvals page → Auto-Send Rules) go out by themselves; everything else waits for you.
- Act Mode: every reply is sent automatically.
Anything touching your business type's always-escalate topics (for example a medical emergency for a clinic) always waits for a person, whatever the level.

## The main pages
- Command Center: today's drafts to review, overdue payments, channel activity.
- Conversations: every chat and call with a customer, in one timeline.
- Approvals: AI replies waiting for your OK.
- Customers, Leads, Commitment Ledger, Campaigns, Automations, Knowledge & Gaps, Analytics, Team, Billing, Settings.
Some pages (Calendar, Scheduling, Queue, Orders, Quotations, Claims, Cases, Properties, Forms, Signals) appear only when that feature is on for your business - see "Features" in the Settings article.

## Who can see what
Owner and admins see everything. Agents (staff) work the inbox, approvals and escalations. Billing, Team, Settings, Analytics, Automations and Campaigns are for the owner and admins only.
