# The KROVA phone app

## Installing it
Open the app link on your phone (Settings in the app shows it, with a Copy button) and add it to your Home Screen. On iPhone, add it to the Home Screen first - notifications only work from there. Team members sign in with their Team ID.

## Tabs
Team members: Today, Inbox, Ledger, Approvals and More. Owner and admins: Today, Inbox, Ask KROVA (the round button in the middle), Ledger and More - Approvals is in More and on Today's "AI replies waiting" card, and its count shows on the Today tab. More holds everything else; screens for features your business doesn't use don't appear.

## Ask KROVA (owner and admins)
Ask anything about your business in Hindi, Hinglish or English - from the round Ask button, or the "KROVA se kuch bhi poochho" bar at the top of Today (tap a ready question or type your own). It answers from KROVA's own records: money owed and received, any customer by name or number, bookings, orders, leads, calls, the team - for today, a week, a month or the dates you name.

## Today
"Needs you now" lists only what is waiting, with a count: escalations, customers waiting for a reply, AI replies to review and calls to write up - or "All clear". Below it: today's bookings and queue (when those are on), and Money - what customers owe you, what is overdue (money owed to you only; your own overdue promises to customers show on a separate line), and the wallet for owner and admins. Owner and admins also get the Ask KROVA bar and shortcuts. Team members get an Available / Away button at the top; money shows only if the owner allows it (Team page → What agents can also do). The number your calls ring on is set in Settings → Your phone (Today asks for it until it is set). Today refreshes when you come back to the app.

## Inbox and chats
- Inbox: search names, numbers or anything ever said; filter All, Waiting (the customer wrote last), Mine or Unassigned. A dot marks customers waiting for a reply; "Template only" means their 24-hour WhatsApp window has closed. The list refreshes by itself.
- A chat opens at the latest message, shows new messages and AI drafts as they arrive, and separates days (Today, Yesterday, a date). AI drafts waiting for approval sit just above the reply box.
- Reply, approve or edit AI drafts, take over a chat, call the customer (your phone rings first).
- When the customer hasn't written in 24 hours, the reply box becomes "Send a template": pick an approved template, fill its blanks, check the preview, send.
- Saved replies: the bookmark button puts one in the reply box.
- Payment link: on an open promise in the chat or on the Ledger.
- Tap the customer's name for their screen: stage, tags, notes, key dates, marketing consent (only the owner or an admin can change consent).

## Customers
Search finds anyone by name or phone. Owner and admins can add a contact (adding doesn't message them).

## More
More is grouped into Your day, Your business and You. Screens for features your business doesn't use don't appear - for example Bookings shows only when "Appointments & staff calendar" is on (Settings → Features on a computer), and Queue only with the walk-in queue.

## Screens by business type (in More)
- Bookings: the day's appointments - They came, No-show, reschedule into an open slot, cancel, payment link, new booking. Tick tests from your fee list on a new booking, or add them to one with "Add tests".
- Queue: open or close a shift, Call next, Done, Skip, check someone in (tick tests from your fee list as you do). It refreshes by itself.
- Leads: newest first; "Not answered yet" shows the ones nobody has replied to. Call or open the chat in one tap.
- Orders: find an order, see COD and delivery state, change the status. To take a new order, open the customer's chat and press "Take an order". An unpaid WhatsApp order shows "Payment link" to send the customer a link for its total.
- Quotations: open quotes, quietest first; mark Won or Lost.

## For owner and admins (in More)
- Business at a glance: wallet, money owed, reply speed this week, how the AI is doing, who on the team is available.
- Automations: switch a rule off or on and see what it did. Building rules is on a computer.
- KROVA commands: change a setting by typing it ("kal 2 se 4 band karo"); you see a preview and confirm.
- Campaigns and broadcasts are on the desktop Campaigns page.
- Help: KROVA Buddy and tickets.

## Notifications
Settings in the app → turn notifications on, then choose which ones you want: escalations, chats given to you, AI replies waiting, customers waiting too long, automation alerts, calls coming to you, wallet. Each person chooses for themselves.

## What stays on a computer
Connecting WhatsApp and Instagram, voice KYC and numbers, building automations and campaigns, creating templates, Flows, integrations, choosing a plan and adding money to the wallet.
