# WhatsApp

## Connecting a WhatsApp number
WhatsApp page → Connect WhatsApp Number. This opens Meta's own Embedded Signup window; you sign in with Facebook, pick or create your WhatsApp Business account and verify the number. The account stays yours. When it finishes, the page shows "Number registered" and "Webhook subscribed". KROVA does not connect WhatsApp by scanning a QR code.
- Moving a number from another provider: WhatsApp page → Migrate a Number. Someone must hold that phone to read the verification code. Quality rating, messaging tier and approved templates come across with it.
- Meta requires a payment method on your WhatsApp Business account before anything can send. If the Command Center says "WhatsApp messages are not sending", add one in Meta Billing (Settings → WhatsApp Account Health → Open Meta Billing).

## Templates
A template is a message Meta approves in advance, so you can send it any time - even outside the 24-hour window. Campaigns and many automations need one.
- Create: WhatsApp page → New Template. Choose a category: Utility (about something the customer already did or has coming - an order, a booking) or Marketing (an offer, a reason to come back; costs more per message). Labelling a promotional message as Utility is against Meta's policy and can hurt your quality rating.
- Variables are written {{1}}, {{2}}... Give realistic example values - Meta's reviewer reads them.
- Can't write one? In New Template, use "Describe it, KROVA writes it": say who gets it and what it should tell them (English or Hinglish), press "Write it for me", and KROVA fills the whole form - name, category, message, examples, buttons. Read it, change anything, then Submit to Meta. Each business gets a fixed number of these a month.
- Status: Pending means Meta is still reviewing (usually under 24 hours). Only Approved templates can be sent. If a template doesn't show its real status, press Sync Meta Templates.
- Start from an example: KROVA's own features (reminders, queue, orders) send templates under fixed names. Keep the name and the numbering if you change the wording, so the feature finds it.
- Carousel templates: WhatsApp page → New Carousel. Up to 10 swipeable cards; the AI can draft the text, the pictures are your own.

## Other WhatsApp tools
- Saved Replies: text you send as-is (refund policy, address).
- Click-to-Chat Link & QR Code: a link and QR that open a chat with your number, with a message already typed.
- WhatsApp Flows: forms that open inside the chat (booking, lead form). Build one, publish it once it validates, then send it.
- 24h Window & Direct Send: check whether a customer's window is open and send a free-text message or a template.
- Business Profile (Settings → Channels): the photo, about line, description, address and category customers see.
- Account Health (Settings → Channels): quality rating, daily limit and status as Meta reports them. Quality falls before a restriction.
- Two-step verification PIN (Settings → Channels): change the number's 6-digit PIN.
