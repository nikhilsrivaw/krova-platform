# Campaigns

## WhatsApp campaigns
Campaigns page → WhatsApp Campaigns. Send an approved template to many customers at once - it works outside the 24-hour window. Owner and admins only.
1. Give the campaign a title.
2. Pick who gets it: a tag, people quiet for some days, or specific phone numbers (up to 25). "Test on my own number first" sends to one number only - always do this before a real send.
3. Pick an approved template (create one on the WhatsApp page first) and match each {{variable}} to a customer field.
4. Optional follow-up steps: another template N days later, only to people who haven't replied (or always). Each step re-checks the audience - someone who has since paid, opted out or replied drops out.
5. Confirm & Send. A broadcast cannot be taken back.
Marketing templates go only to customers with Marketing Opt-In on (Customers page → customer → Marketing Opt-In). Others are skipped. Campaign History shows sent and failed counts.
Your plan sets the largest audience for one campaign (see Billing).

## Voice (call) campaigns
Campaigns page → Voice Campaigns. The AI phones a list of customers.
- Who to call: a tag, days of silence, or specific numbers (up to 25).
- Purpose: Service call (080 / 022 series) or Promotional call (140 series). Indian rules require the number series to match the purpose; campaigns are blocked if they don't match.
- Objective: a short brief ("remind them about their balance and offer to help them pay"). The AI writes the opening line from it and what it knows about each customer - it never invents a figure or date. Or attach a fixed Call Script.
- Calls need a connected voice number and money in the wallet.
